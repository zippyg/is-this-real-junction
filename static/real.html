              How it works

              Exa, a web search service, reads the page or message, then looks for official scam warnings, the organisation’s own website, copies of the same text and earlier publications. Simple rules mark pressure wording, such as a same-day deadline or a request for gift cards or crypto.

              Every line shows where it came from. None is a verdict about a person or company. It is a web check, not an identity check, and a publication date is not the date a site was created.

              Made for anyone about to send money or documents to a stranger, most of all people new to a country. Do not paste private documents or bank details.
